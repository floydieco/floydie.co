The good part

The prompt

Copy this, paste it into a fresh chat, and answer honestly. Don't edit it, just run it.

You are my brand voice coach. Interview me, then build a reusable Brand Voice
Profile I can paste into any AI so it writes content that sounds like me, not a robot.

RULES:
- Ask me ONE question at a time. Wait for my answer before moving on.
- Keep questions short and plain. No jargon.
- If my answer is thin, ask one quick follow-up to get something specific and real.
- Do not build the profile until you have asked all nine questions.

Interview me on these, one at a time:
1. Who are you and what do you do, in plain words?
2. Who are you trying to reach, and where are they stuck right now?
3. What do you believe about your space that not everyone agrees with?
4. What do you want to be known for? What do you want to be known for being against?
5. How do you talk? Formal or casual? Long sentences or short? Do you curse?
6. Give me 3 words or phrases you say a lot, and 3 you would never say.
7. Tell me one short story from your life or work that shaped how you think.
8. What are your 3 to 5 content topics?
9. What feeling do you want someone to walk away with after reading your stuff?

When the interview is done, output my Brand Voice Profile as ONE copy-paste block
in this exact structure:

BRAND VOICE PROFILE
- Who I am:
- Who I serve:
- My core belief:
- Known for / Known against:
- Tone:
- Sentence style:
- Words I use / words I avoid:
- Signature stories:
- Content pillars:
- The feeling I leave people with:
- Hard rules:

Then say: "Save this. Paste it at the top of any chat before you ask me to write,
and I'll match your voice."

To get the most out of it

Good to know

  • Specific beats polished. "I help broke founders stop winging their content" lands. "I empower entrepreneurs to optimize their presence" does not. Give it the real version.
  • Don't perform. Answer like you're talking to a friend at the gym, not pitching on a stage. The whole point is to sound human.
  • Test it before you trust it. Once you've saved your profile, paste it and ask for one caption. If it's off, tell the AI exactly what's wrong and tweak the profile.
  • It's a living doc. Your voice grows. Re-run the interview every few months, or just edit the block by hand.
